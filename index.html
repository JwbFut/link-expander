Link Expander

Plenty of people try to make links shorter. So what if one is longer?

Open it to check that it works

How does it work?

Only a handful of characters carry your destination and the seed; everything else is noise derived from that seed, padding the link out to the exact length you asked for. The same destination, seed and length always rebuild the identical link, so nothing is lost on the way — and opening the link goes to your destination.

Up to 8192 characters works. Past 2000, some browsers, chat apps and mail clients start truncating, and the page warns you when you go that far. The destination has to be an http:// or https:// link.
